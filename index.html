Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 7, 2026

1. Information We Collect

MindTrade collects data you enter directly, including trade records, emotion scores, and bias tags. All data is stored locally on your device and is never transmitted to external servers.

2. Data Storage & Security

Your data is stored in local device storage (SwiftData). If you use the iCloud Backup feature, your data is stored in encrypted form in your personal iCloud account.

3. In-App Purchases

Premium subscriptions are processed through RevenueCat. Payment information is managed by Apple — the app never has direct access to your payment details.

4. Third-Party Sharing

We do not sell or share your data with any third parties.
